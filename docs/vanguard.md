## II. The Reality Check: The Laser Pulse in Flight

The starkest divergence between our Process Ontology and academic operationalism is illustrated by a simple physical experiment: **pulsing a laser into the vacuum of space.**

* **The Operationalist Hand-Wave (Copenhagen & Instrumentalism):**  
  Standard textbook quantum mechanics claims that because light is detected in discrete "clicks" at an atom, the photon only exists at emission and absorption. In transit, it is treated as an abstract mathematical wave function $\psi(x,t)$ evolving in a complex Hilbert space. Extreme operationalists argue that asking what the light is doing between the laser and the target is "unphysical" because no measurement is taking place. If the laser is switched off, the packet in flight is treated as a disembodied probability mist.
* **The Planck Field Realism:**  
  When a laser fires, real physical momentum is injected into the network. When the laser shuts off, that momentum **does not stop**, does not freeze, and does not dissolve into a mathematical abstraction. It is an active, deterministic, sequential train of relational state hand-offs cascading across discrete nodes at the network's maximum causal throughput ($v = c = 1\text{ link/tick}$). It is physically moving through the substrate whether a human observer, detector, or mirror ever intercepts it.

We agree with the vanguard that light is not a stream of tiny flying marbles; but we reject the defeatist claim that light in flight is unphysical. It is **unobstructed moving momentum** propagating across an active, memoryless grid.

### Pillar 1: Gerard 't Hooft — The Discrete Cellular Substrate

* **Primary Works:**  
  * *The Cellular Automaton Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics* (Fundamental Theories of Physics, Springer, 2016)
  * *"Entangled quantum states in a local deterministic theory"* (2009)
* **The Breakthrough (Where We Agree):**  
  't Hooft demonstrated that the apparent "mysteries" of quantum mechanics—superposition, wave-particle duality, and the Born probability rule ($P = |\psi|^2$)—do not require genuine indeterminism or mystical observer-dependent reality. At the Planck scale ($10^{-35}\text{ m}, 5.4 \times 10^{-44}\text{ s}$), nature is an entirely deterministic, discrete cellular automaton. Quantum states are mathematical equivalence classes: because macroscopic instruments cannot track $10^{43}$ deterministic updates per second, we are forced to group vast microstate trajectories into statistical probabilistic vectors.
* **The Point of Divergence (Where We Disagree):**  
  To make his theory palatable to mainstream academic physicists, 't Hooft spends enormous mathematical effort mapping discrete integer automaton states back onto continuous complex Hilbert spaces ($\mathbb{C}$), continuous global time, and unitary Hamiltonian evolution ($e^{-iHt}$). In doing so, he re-introduces the very continuous mathematical baggage that causes infinities.
* **The Planck Field Realization:**  
  We discard the complex Hilbert space detour. The Planck Field is a strictly finite, integer-valued network. State transitions are local, memoryless updates ($t \to t+1$) governed by integer capacity limits (`FLOW_CAP`, `DEADLOCK`). We do not need continuous complex wave functions to explain physics; statistical interference patterns emerge natively as arrival-time combs and ensemble histograms (measured on the single-spin prototype).

### Pillar 2: Willis Lamb & Art Hobson — The Non-Existence of Point Particles

* **Primary Works:**  
  * Willis E. Lamb, Jr., *"Anti-photon"*, *Applied Physics B* 60, 77–84 (1995)
  * Art Hobson, *"There are no particles, there are only fields"*, *American Journal of Physics* 81, 211 (2013)
  * Roy J. Glauber, *Quantum Theory of Optical Coherence* (Nobel Prize Lecture, 2005)
* **The Breakthrough (Where We Agree):**  
  Lamb and Hobson systematically dismantled the Newtonian "billiard ball" view of light. A photon is not a microscopic bullet localized to a zero-dimensional point. In quantum optics, quantization belongs to the **field mode**, not an isolated corpuscle. A single optical photon ($\lambda \approx 500\text{ nm}$) is an extended phase-correlated wave packet spanning roughly $3 \times 10^{28}$ Planck lengths along its trajectory and expanding laterally across millions of nodes. The localized "particle" behavior occurs strictly at the detector boundary, where energy is exchanged in discrete atomic packets.
* **The Point of Divergence (Where We Disagree):**  
  Lamb retreated into an operationalist stance, famously declaring that photons don't exist and that physics should speak only of classical electromagnetic fields exciting quantized matter. Hobson similarly rested on continuous differential field equations. This leaves an ontological vacuum: if a photon isn't a particle, what is physically traveling through space when a laser pulses?
* **The Planck Field Realization:**  
  Light in transit is an un-anchored, linear train of discrete relational momentum quanta cascading along open vacuum links at $v=1$. It is not a zero-dimensional ball, nor an abstract equation; it is active momentum propagating across a discrete graph. When it strikes an active matter boundary, local capacity saturation triggers a discrete avalanche (the Avalanche Photodiode mechanism measured on the single-spin prototype), producing the illusion of a localized "click."

### Pillar 3: Carlo Rovelli — Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM)

* **Primary Works:**  
  * Carlo Rovelli, *"Relational Quantum Mechanics"*, *International Journal of Theoretical Physics* 35, 1637–1678 (1996)

* **The Breakthrough (Where We Agree):**  
  Rovelli directly attacked the foundation of substance ontology: the assumption that reality is composed of independent "substances" possessing static, intrinsic properties while floating inside a passive spatial container. * **The Point of Divergence (Where We Disagree):**  
  Rovelli’s relational interpretation often veers into epistemic anti-realism. This obscures the physical mechanism behind infinite mathematical integrals that require artificial renormalization to cancel.

## IV. Methodological Integrity: The Scale Gap & The Role of the Simulator

$$\text{Direct 1:1 Simulation of } 1\text{ mm}^3 \text{ for } 100\text{ ms} \implies 10^{137} \text{ discrete operations}$$
