# qi3 Method — Building Quantum Structure from Local Mechanics

How we built the quantized observables (charge, spin, Pauli,
winding) from Planck-scale local causal mechanics — the
process that worked.

## The methodology

1. **Start from measurement, not theory.** Don't ask "what
   should the model have" — ask "what do physicists actually
   measure?" Every target was a real observable (charge in e,
   spin in hbar, Pauli exclusion), not a theoretical belief.

2. **Decompose the observable to local mechanics.** For each
   gap, ask: what LOCAL process produces it? Charge isn't a
   "quantum number" — it's a count of a conserved unit.
   Pauli isn't an "exclusion principle" — it's a mode being
   single-occupancy. Spin isn't a "rotation" — it's a
   circulation-count.

3. **Find the conserved/topological structure.** The
   quantum observables all turned out to be COUNTS:
   - charge = count of conserved circulation-units (vortices)
   - spin = count of circulation-quanta (net-dir/rung)
   - winding = net flux around a closed path
   Counts are naturally discrete — the quantization is
   emergent, not imposed.

4. **Ground it in the substrate's existing mechanics.** The
   rung-quantization (loops only form at 32,64,96,128) IS
   the quantum — circulation in indivisible chunks. The
   directedness (a-b) IS the flux-gradient = the phase.
   The per-channel modes ARE the single-occupancy states.

5. **Verify it's a mechanism, not a patch.** Each addition
   had to conserve the ledger, stay local, and produce the
   observable WITHOUT being imposed. If it needs a global
   rule or an explicit quantum-number field, it's wrong —
   the structure must emerge from local capacity/counts.

## What worked

- The flux-gradient reframe: directedness IS the phase-
  gradient — the circulation IS the winding (no separate
  phase DOF needed).
- Per-direction single-occupancy: a channel's + mode
  saturating at one quantum gives Pauli exclusion — same-
  direction bound states coincide, opposite coexist.
- Vortex count as charge: bound states carry integer
  numbers of conserved circulation-quanta — a real
  topological charge.
- Circulation-count as spin: net-directedness/rung = the
  quantized angular-momentum count.

## The honest principle

The "quantum" structure isn't magic — it's what local
causal processes produce when the substrate's mechanics
are right. The postulates (charge, spin, Pauli) are
topological/count structures, not axioms.
