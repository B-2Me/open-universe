# Substrate Ontology

*What the engine mechanically expresses — measured, not asserted.*

This is the Map (what exists) and the Compass (which way the loops
run) for the `moore26` engine, distilled from the measured record in
[Moore26 Findings](/moore26-findings). Every claim below traces to a
harness measurement; nothing is an authored force unless flagged.
*(The meso3i engine's map is preserved in the
[archive](/interference/archive/ontology).)*

## The Map — what exists

**The vacuum is a ledger, not a void.** "Empty" is a defined state
(zero registers), not an absence — measured null: inert cells show
0 fluctuations, bare mass is silent. Deviations from this state are
the substrate's only substance.

**Deviation = winding.** A signed, per-channel circulation count —
the record of departure from empty. It comes in two grades that
magnitude alone hides: *wear* (traffic scars, no provenance) and
*committed bonds* (timestamped structure). The deviation-age
spectrum (`bondTick` distribution) discriminates them — measured.

**Quanta = packets.** `{count, nu, parity, born}` riding the 12 edge
channels of the even sublattice. `nu` is one register carrying both
energy and clock-rate (the substrate's h=1): free frequency, energy
in flight, *no address* — the photon-like case.

**Bound structure = mass + winding on the matter lattice.** Odd
cells are the sustained state — mass is the parked condensation
register; committed winding is deviation trapped into circulation.
The two parities split the ontology: even cells transport and
record transactions; odd cells *are*.

**Mass = confined frequency.** Not a separate scalar, and not
interchangeable with energy — the photon counterexample holds
here too (packets have rate but no bound cycle). The structure's
characteristic frequency `geo_gap` is its mass readout, measurable
only through what it re-emits — the same operational content as
nature's decay-product mass reconstruction. *Mass is energy with
an address: nu bound into a cycle that cannot leave.*

**Time is granular.** `qTick` is the drumbeat (ballistic period
exactly 3 on rail rings, bath-immune — no dilation exists for
transport). `localTime` is a proper-time accumulator — quiet vs
congested twins diverge 22×; under `qMassCap`, heavier structure
ticks *faster* (mass = accelerated time; mass=0 → no proper time
at all).

**Stochasticity lives at the tick.** Every gate is a per-tick
Bernoulli roll (`rand % qCatchGate >= lvl`). Deterministic
transport + per-instant chance = the ensemble distributions we
measure — the granularity is the sampling substrate that produces
probability, not a defect of it.

**History is a register, not a narrative.** `bondTick` (first-
confirm birthday), `workAge` (transaction effort), `bondPar`
(chirality record), `bondDist` (bonded reach). Provenance is
local grid state — which is what makes it *visual*: a moving
mass's trajectory is literally written as a trail of birthdays.

**Identity is not stored — it is reconstructed.** Relocation is a
complete handoff (odd→odd through the even conduit, 1 cell/tick);
every vacated site writes `m=1` + a fresh birthday. No register
says "the same thing passed here" — we (and any future reader)
connect the timestamped sites and call it a trajectory. Same
object as a bubble-chamber track.

## The Compass — which way the loops run

- **Transaction**: offer → incipient → confirm, every tick. An
  offer arms on *arrival* (seeding on-cell skips arming), the
  catch gate rolls per tick, confirms deposit winding + write
  birthdays.
- **Emission**: `site_unwind` sheds a rung; the packet carries
  `nu = shed/2` (conserving) or `nu = geo_gap` under `qResEmit`
  (the spectral line).
- **Stimulated branch**: an offer at `nu == geo_gap` flags the
  incipient; the stimulator *transmits*, the clone exits
  collimated at the line, phase-locked to the offer's epoch —
  funded by exactly `line` of committed winding. Gain ~3×
  measured.
- **Decay**: winding erodes via spontaneous emission; the
  weakest committed pair drifts `geo_gap` — the drift IS the
  ensemble linewidth (~20%, measured comb).
- **Accretion**: feed → wear → confirm → committed + birthday.
  Grown structure is stratified (birthdays spread over the feed
  window, surviving decay); authored `winding_set` structure is
  ageless — detectably different in kind.
- **Motion**: bare mass relays under momentum (complete handoff);
  committed structure cannot hop — the speed-structure exclusion
  (a wound knot can't dwell to form AND can't move while wound).

## What the substrate does not have (honest list)

- **No amplitudes** — the merge gate (`qMergeGate`) is authored
  phase arbitration, binary admission; not wave arithmetic.
- **No far-field scattering** — interactions are capture +
  re-emission; no angular amplitude exists to Fourier-invert yet.
- **No Lorentz invariance** — transport is absolute; dilation
  keys on load, not velocity.
- **No per-species lifetimes** — decay is structure-dependent
  drift, not an exponential constant.
- **No thing-table** — and that is the point, not the gap.
