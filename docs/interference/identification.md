## Two candidate identifications

**A — Primordial Planck vacuum.** The substrate is spacetime itself at
the Planck epoch: lumps are topological defects of the lattice (geons,
vortex knots), `n` is local oscillator occupation, the photon field is
the primordial radiation bath. Under this reading, real atoms and
laboratory clocks are statistical ensembles 10²⁰–10³⁰ scales above the
grid — the substrate can never be "a proton" because a proton is a
coarse-grained object built of ~10¹⁹ lattice spacings.

**B — Condensed-matter analog.** The lattice is a crystal; `iE` is a
vibrational quantum (phonon occupation), photons are polariton-like
propagating modes, lumps are self-trapped defects (polarons,
skyrmions), and the E23 filament is ordinary phonon conduction. This
reading is weaker as cosmology but stronger as physics — every
mechanism has an established solid-state precedent.

## What would settle it

- **A discriminating prediction.** An experiment where the two
  readings give different answers — e.g., does the equilibrium photon
  density scale like a blackbody spectrum under density change
  (Planck-vacuum reading) or like a Debye phonon gas bounded by the
  lattice's channel cap (crystal reading)?
- **A falsifiable constant.** If any measured substrate number
  (decay width, detention factor, equilibrium density) can be pinned
  to a dimensionless real-world ratio, the bridge graduates from
  "units by construction" to "a constant of the system."
- ~~The missing leg.~~ **Closed (E27, `29c4ec0`).** `IPHOT>=8`
  fires Breit-Wheeler on the lattice: antipodal photon pairs at a
  shared site crystallize into rest mass — head-on beams in vacuum
  produce real `rest` (control: zero). The mass↔photon↔`n` triangle
  is now fully closed — every energy form interconverts with
  integer conservation.
