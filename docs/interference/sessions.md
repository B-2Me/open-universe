# Planned Audio Sessions

*Note: The audio roadmap is a living syllabus. The exact order and topics are tentative, but each episode will be anchored by specific media (films, shows, or lectures) that you can watch beforehand to set the context.*

## Episode 01: The Concurrency of Scale (Introduction)
* **Anchor Media:** *Powers of Ten* (1977 Documentary Short)
* **Focus:** Introducing the framework and the author. We explore how spending a career as a software engineer hunting nanosecond concurrency bugs teaches a hard truth: human intuition is completely blind to scale. We explore the sheer size of the Planck Field, the absolute speed limit of causality ($c$), and why treating the universe like a synchronized global state (a "block") physically breaks down at the fundamental resolution.

## Episode 02: The Latency Buffer & The Swerve
* **Anchor Media:** *Devs* (Mini-series)
* **Focus:** Directly expanding on **[Audit 4 (The Calorimetry of Agency)](/interference/audits)**. We look at the thermodynamic exertion required to break the "tramlines" of determinism. We explore how the absolute speed of causality ($c$) provides the physical latency necessary for free will, giving the biological interface the breathing room to simulate, predict, and ultimately swerve.

## Episode 03: Your Memories are Scars, Not Time Machines
* **Anchor Media:** *Memento* (Film)
* **Focus:** An examination of how biological systems loop. Memory is not accessing a saved file from the past; it is the act of touching present-day physical neural clay. We explore how this mechanism creates the illusion of historical time travel, and why mistaking scars for time machines traps consciousness in deterministic habits.

## Episode 04: Internal Depth vs. External Depth (The Geometric Dashboard)
* **Anchor Media:** *The Matrix* (Film)
* **Focus:** Unpacking the mathematician's dilemma. We explore the difference between *external depth* (the brute-force, fundamental thermodynamic accounting of the field) and *internal depth* (the breathtaking complexity of the biological interface rendering 3D spatial geometry). We discuss why geometry is the ultimate sensory compression, proving that mathematicians aren't mapping the empty void: they are mapping the highest-level cognitive architecture of the human mind.
