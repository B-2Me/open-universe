1. Lattice & Channel Layout

The grid is a cubic cell lattice carrying the FCC even-parity sub-lattice: transactions arm only on even-parity cells (x+y+z)&1==0. Each node owns QChannels=26 directional channels — one per Moore-stencil neighbor — mapped onto the rhombic-dodecahedral FCC coordination cell (octahedral symmetry Oh).

ChannelsRoleNeighbors

The 12 edge channels are the only packet-flight channels — each points at an FCC nearest neighbor. Bound structure (winding) lives on all 26 channels as directed pairs (2e, 2e+1) — a forward/backward ledger per axis.

3. Tick Pipeline

One tick executes the following local phases in order, single-buffered into grid_next:

1

Transport v/c = 1.00

Every packet hops exactly one edge channel to its neighbor — the literal speed of light. Free quanta are dispersion-free; the front advances +1 hop per tick.

2

Arm (offer)

A packet arriving at an even cell arms an incipient transaction if it passes the gates: catch gate, on-band line match (nu % line == 0, line = (mass + winding>>2)>>2), phase gate, and channel occupancy. Off-band packets fly on and eventually decay to heat (ev7).

3

Confirm & deposit

Held incipients confirm (session maturity via incipBorn). The confirmed nu splits three ways:

  • share = nu·vol_share>>8 — volumetric deposit onto the dominant member of the top committed edges (direction-following)
  • dep = nu − share — lands on the arrival pair, split by signed dir_bias (>0 forward/travel, <0 toward the offerer)
  • cnu = nu·chain_share>>8 — the chained re-offer
4

Charge-response feed conserving

The dominant directed edge absorbs feed = dd·nu>>9 redirected from the biased deposit member — momentum feeds circulation without minting winding. (Pre-fix this term was added on top of the deposit — the pump that drove the dimer to 7.7M.)

5

Chained re-offer + surplus toll conserving

The confirm re-offers a packet on the dominant-dd channel — the signal hops node-to-node along the structure's own rail. The re-offer is funded by a surplus toll: it draws only the margin above the pair's committed floor min(a,b). The bond is protected; traffic pays for traffic.

6

Emission (structure → radiation)

Excited sites (level ≥ 2) shed a rung via site_unwind: with res_emit=1 the packet carries nu = line (the site's own frequency — resonant emission); else nu = shed>>1. Channel pick prefers bond → dominant-dd (chiral/dipole emission) → random. A mass-bearing site instead condenses winding→mass when mass×8 < Σ committed (the ~1:3.4 accretion equilibrium).

7

Macroscopic events

String-break: a saturated >128 tube of ≥12 contiguous nodes fractures at the tube head — endpoints pair-produce mass + chargeFlight recoil. Mass-flight transport, charge drift, heat drain (−1/128/tick) close the tick.
