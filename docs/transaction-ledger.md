**The Physical Proof** — The parameter map cleanly isolates `dir_bias` as the primary driver of bound circulation. **The Verdict** — Without a strictly asymmetric deposition rule, the lattice cannot convert linear momentum into bound circulation. `dir_bias` functions as the master switch for the existence of matter on the substrate; without it, topological structures cannot form or persist.

## III. The Surplus Toll: Topological Protection & Conservation

**The Mechanic** — To prevent chained routing from acting as a perpetual-motion spin-drive, the engine exacts a toll on `chain_share`. This toll deducts `cnu` exclusively from the winding above the committed pair floor (`min(a,b)`).

**The Physical Proof** — Under legacy pre-conservation rules (no toll), chained re-offers acted as an unbounded pump, driving a knot's `dd` to 7.7 million. The "surplus toll" resolved this entirely: by deducting only from the uncommitted margin, the dimer established a perfectly stable `dd = 519` plateau.

**The Verdict** — The surplus toll formally partitions the discrete ledger into two physical regimes: a protected structural track (the locked, committed bond) and a consumable signal margin (the transient flux). This rule ensures that propagating signals pay their required thermodynamic friction costs exclusively from ambient noise, preventing them from eroding the foundational structure they ride on.
