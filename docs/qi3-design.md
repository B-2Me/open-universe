## What qi3 adds (the absorber side — where QM lives)

link3's gap was absorber response: lumps had ONE excitation register (iE —
a scalar bank). Real absorption is selective: a transition either completes
at a matching energy or doesn't happen. qi3's sites carry an internal
LEVEL STRUCTURE:

### Registers (spelled-out naming — long names document the ontology;
### inner-loop helpers may stay shorthand)

- `qLevel[site]` — occupation state: which internal mode the site
  occupies (integer n — the level number, or bitmask of occupied modes)
- `qCharge[site]` — bound charge (deposits from completed transactions)
- `qConfirm[site][c]` — pending confirmations per channel: offers
  received, responses armed (the TI's confirmation wave made a register)
- level gap structure: the transition energies this site can express
  (gaps between allowed configurations — the spectrum is generated by
  the structure, not hardcoded)

### The transaction (replaces absorb-on-contact)

1. Photon (offer, ν) transits a massive site
2. Site checks its gap table: does ν match an allowed transition?
   - exact match → arm a CONFIRMATION on the reverse channel
   - no match → photon passes (the offer declined — no partial fills)
3. Confirmation returns to emitter-region / completes the transaction:
   photon absorbed, absorber level rises n'→n
4. Emission: a site in level n can fall n→n' emitting ν = gap(n,n') —
   the photon's ν IS the level difference (spectrum = structure)
